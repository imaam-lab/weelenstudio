About Weelen Studio

A freelance creative and web design/development studio.

Design that helps businesses look professional and work well online.

Weelen Studio is a freelance creative and web design/development company. It provides digital design, branding, web design and development, social media management and related creative services.

The work centres on professional design, a strong digital presence and custom solutions, with each project built around the client’s needs.

What we focus on

Six areas shape how Weelen Studio works with every client.

Professional design

Clean, considered work that presents your business with confidence.

Digital presence

Websites and social media that represent you consistently online.

Branding

Logos and visual identities that make a business recognisable.

Web experiences

Responsive, easy-to-use websites built around your content.

Custom solutions

Work shaped around each client rather than a fixed formula.

Client-focused work

Your goals and feedback guide every stage of a project.

How we work

A simple, transparent workflow keeps every project clear from start to finish.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start by learning about your business, audience and goals.

  2. 02

    Understand

    We clarify requirements, content and preferences before any design begins.

  3. 03

    Design

    We create concepts and layouts for your review.

  4. 04

    Develop

    We build websites and prepare production-ready files.

  5. 05

    Refine

    We adjust the details based on your feedback.

  6. 06

    Deliver

    We hand over final files or launch your website.
