Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply if I don't see a suitable opening?

Yes. Choose "General Application" as your position and describe the kind of work you'd like to do.

Can I apply as a beginner?

Yes. Select "No professional experience yet" or "Less than 1 year", and share what you're learning and building.

Do I need to provide a portfolio?

A portfolio helps us understand your work, but it's optional. Write "Not provided" if you don't have one.

How do I submit my application?

Complete the form and tap "Send to WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with your application ready, and you press send.
